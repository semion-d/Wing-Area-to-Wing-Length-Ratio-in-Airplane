In this case we will not use thrust for seceral reasons: it does not effect efficiency of the airplane itself, it just creates a cointinious kinetic energy input; and it is harder to simulate on a small experimental scale, the only way where trust will be included is the power input, when we throw the airplane. So what's left is Gravity (or weight), Lift, and Drag.
Lift is a force that pushes plane upwards, it happend because the area under the wing has higher airpressure then on the top, and this pressure difference moves airplane up. It is calculated through this formula: 


\[ 
L = \frac{1}{2}pv^2SC_L
\]
